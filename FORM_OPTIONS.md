# Form Options

## Recommendation

Use HubSpot Forms if the client wants a known, professional option with CRM follow-up. It is more recognizable than Formspree for business teams and lets leads go directly into a contact pipeline.

Keep WhatsApp as the fastest CTA.

## Options

### 1. HubSpot Forms
Best for: professional lead capture, CRM, follow-up, contact history.
Pros: known brand, free tier, CRM included, easy embed, good for sales process.
Cons: requires setting up HubSpot account and form embed.

### 2. Google Forms
Best for: maximum familiarity and fastest setup.
Pros: everyone knows it, easy to manage, responses go to Sheets.
Cons: less premium visually, embed can feel generic.

### 3. Typeform
Best for: premium conversational experience.
Pros: polished UX, high perceived quality, easy conditional questions.
Cons: paid limits can matter.

### 4. Tally
Best for: clean form with simple embed and low friction.
Pros: modern, flexible, easier to style than Google Forms.
Cons: less known by non-technical clients than Google/HubSpot.

## Suggested Fields

- Nombre completo
- Empresa
- RUC, optional
- Correo
- WhatsApp
- Rubro
- Motivo de consulta
- Cantidad aproximada de trabajadores, optional
- Mensaje

## Suggested Flow

Primary CTA: WhatsApp.
Secondary CTA: embedded HubSpot form.
Fallback: mailto link to contacto@grupocastaneda.pe.
